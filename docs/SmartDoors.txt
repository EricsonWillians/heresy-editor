Smart Door authoring
====================

Smart Door is a sector-finishing tool.  Draw the intended door sector first,
including its two-sided openings and any one-sided track walls.  In 2D Sector
mode, select one or more nonadjacent door sectors and use Edit -> Make Smart
Door or the Sector Operations menu.  With no selection, the highlighted sector
is used temporarily.  The sector itself becomes the moving door volume: Smart
Door does not choose one of its neighbors.

Review
------

The TARGET box names the exact sector numbers that will be converted, including
when the command temporarily uses the highlighted sector.  The dialog reports
how many sectors, activating portal lines, and track walls the planner found.
The map overlay uses three separate roles:

* orange hatch with a heavy orange outline: sectors that will become doors
* orange lines: portal lines that receive the door action
* green: one-sided track walls

The overlay remains visible beside the review window.  Selecting a warning or
error centers the map on its sector or line.  Nothing is changed until Make
Door is pressed.

Choose a semantic preset such as a normal, fast, stay-open, or locally
supported keyed door.  The available list comes from the active game, source
port, and map format; unsupported representations are hidden.

Door face texture is the surface visible on the moving door slab.  Track wall
texture is the texture on the narrow side walls inside the doorway.  Browse,
or click either preview tile, to open a searchable list of loaded wall textures
with dimensions and a larger preview.  Search is case-insensitive.  Use Auto
to clear an override, or type a custom resource name directly.  A green, lit
Auto button means that field is genuinely using inference; typing or choosing
an override turns it off.  The resolved choice and its purpose are shown below
each control.

Auto face inference prefers neighbor-facing portal surfaces, then track
surfaces, then the configured default wall.  Auto track inference prefers
existing track middles, then the inferred face, then the default wall.  Linedef
length supplies the weight, and the lowest linedef number resolves a tie.  A
texture that is not loaded is a warning rather than an error, so custom
resource names remain possible.

Validation
----------

Every selected sector needs at least two two-sided portal boundaries.  A batch
is blocked if a sector is malformed or self-referencing, if selected door
sectors are adjacent, if heights are invalid, or if portal ownership conflicts.
Warnings call attention to unusual or split shapes, extra neighbors, missing
tracks, low opening clearance, floor mismatches, custom textures, shared
sidedefs that will be isolated, and existing specials that will be replaced.

Make Door remains disabled while any blocking error exists.  The complete
selection is planned again when Make Door is pressed.  If any sector has become
invalid, no sector is changed.

Result and undo
---------------

Portal lines are oriented with the door sector on the back/left, receive the
chosen special, arguments, and map-format activation, and place the Face
texture on the neighbor-facing upper surface.  Track walls become actionless,
one-sided/blocking, lower-unpegged walls using the Track texture.  The door
ceiling closes to its floor.

Sector flats, floor height, lighting, type, tag, offsets, potentially visible
portal lower textures, and unrelated line flags are preserved.  Only affected
shared sidedefs are copied.  The whole batch is one Undo step, and Redo restores
it.  Cancel or closing the dialog makes no edit and does not alter selection.

Boundaries
----------

Smart Door does not draw, split, resize, or move geometry.  Remote switches and
automatic tags, raw special editing, live 3D mutation, animated or sliding
doors, and polyobject construction are outside this tool.  The transient
planning and preview architecture is intended for later lifts, stairs,
corridors, and portal-building tools.

Smart Sector integration
------------------------

Smart Sector Designer now supplies the geometry-building counterpart.  Its
Extrude and Corridor modes can choose a Door connection, which inserts the
door-depth sector and calls this same Smart Door planner and mutation code
inside the geometry operation.  Extrusion can place a centered, sized, and
offset door on the automatically chosen longest source segment, or place
independent doors on explicitly selected segments of a nonbranching chain.
Auto width reserves track walls at chain bends; the remaining extrusion seam
stays a blocking wall.  The embedded Preset, Face, and Track controls have
identical Auto and compatibility behavior, and the embedded face/track preview
tiles and Choose buttons open the same loaded-texture picker.
Generated geometry, portal orientation, textures, special, arguments,
activation, and collapsed ceiling commit as one Undo step; if either geometry
or door revalidation fails, none of the combined operation is retained.  See
`docs/SmartSectors.txt`.
