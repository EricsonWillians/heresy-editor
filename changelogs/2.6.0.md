# Heresy Editor 2.6.0

Heresy Editor 2.6.0 overhauls the 3D view with first-person navigation,
FPS-style mouse-look, and automatic camera recovery, enables UDMF as a
first-class format for new maps and projects, and fixes stale rendering
when creating fresh maps.

## First-person 3D navigation

* Added FPS-style mouse-look, on by default when entering the 3D view:
  the pointer hides and steers the camera — horizontal motion turns,
  vertical motion moves forward and backward — recentering only when it
  nears the canvas edge. Press M or ESC to release the mouse, and M to
  re-engage it. Turn and move sensitivity are configurable in
  Preferences → 3D View (`render_mlook_turn` / `render_mlook_move`).
* Popular movement semantics: WASD moves, SHIFT runs at 2× speed, SPACE
  jumps (fly up), and left CTRL crouches (fly down) — all continuous
  while held, and all usable while steering with the mouse. Left and
  right CTRL are now bindable keys, and the WASD bindings accept held
  modifiers.
* Camera recovery: the camera starts at the player start on fresh maps,
  re-homes itself when entering the 3D view with stale out-of-bounds
  coordinates, and H (or HOME) teleports it back to the player start at
  any time.
* Void visualization: when the camera leaves the map, the background
  turns black and the whole level is drawn as a theme-colored wireframe,
  X-ray style, so it is easy to fly back inside. (OpenGL renderer.)
* A controls hint is shown in the status bar when entering the 3D view,
  and the default bindings file documents the full control scheme.

## UDMF for new maps and projects

* UDMF is now offered in the Map Type choice of New Project and Manage
  Project for ports that advertise UDMF support (currently zdoom and
  eternity), and TEXTMAP levels are always recognized when loading.
  The hidden `udmftest` command-line option is deprecated.

## Fixes

* Creating a fresh map (File → Fresh Map, or Create Next Map) no longer
  leaves the previous map behind: its geometry stayed rendered, the
  selection and highlight referenced old objects, render caches and
  side panels survived, and the 3D camera was stranded in the void.
