# Heresy Editor 2.5.0

Heresy Editor 2.5.0 makes the construction grid legible at a glance, adds
real-world measurement readouts throughout the editor, and removes the
16-bit ceiling on sector heights for UDMF maps.

## Grid visibility themes

* Added High-Contrast Dark, Vintage Phosphor, Blueprint Light, and Custom
  grid visibility themes. Fine, reference, major, axis, origin, snap-target,
  halo, and pointer-guide roles each own a distinct weight and color across
  square, Dotty, and mathematical grids.
* Every theme coordinates the map ink itself: the grid always stays far
  weaker than geometry — dim on the dark canvases, pale on the light one —
  while walls, linedefs, things, vertices, selection, and snapping each own
  a separate high-contrast hue band that cannot be mistaken for the grid.
* Dense grids fade toward the canvas as lines pack together, dissolving to a
  faint texture instead of veiling the map; sub-pixel grids are simplified
  instead of becoming a solid tint.
* Added a grid opacity slider (20-100%) that fades grid lines toward the
  canvas on any theme.
* The snap reticle now renders above map geometry as an outlined top-layer
  marker with a short guide back to the raw pointer position.
* Camera, error, tagged-feedback, sound-blocking, and sound-propagation
  colors follow the active theme, and the Preferences guidance box reports
  the measured worst-case contrast for the selected theme. Preferences →
  Grid retains complete Custom color control with the classic map object
  colors.

## Real-world measurement

* Added optional metric and imperial measurement readouts with a
  configurable map-units-per-meter scale (classic Doom convention: 32 units
  ≈ 1 m). Lengths auto-scale for readability ("85 cm", "16.0 m", "52.5 ft").
* The linedef info overlay shows the exact length on the side of the line
  opposite the mouse cursor, clamped to the canvas.
* The status bar reports the live width, height, and real-world size of the
  selection rectangle while rubber-band selecting, and keeps a persistent
  right-aligned map dimension readout for the loaded level.
* Grid size buttons size themselves to their longest label, so wide spacings
  with unit suffixes no longer spill over neighboring controls.

## Extreme sector heights (UDMF)

* Sector floor and ceiling heights are no longer clamped to the binary
  format's -32767..32767 range when editing UDMF maps. The sector panel,
  headroom field, and the floor/ceiling raise-lower commands now follow the
  active map format: int16 for Doom/Hexen, full 32-bit range for UDMF.
* BiasedDoom accepts UDMF heights through ±262144, so tall-and-deep
  architecture can be authored directly in the editor.
* Saving a binary-format map with out-of-range heights now warns explicitly
  and points to UDMF instead of silently truncating the stored values.
* The 3D view far-clip preference adds 65536, 262144, and 1048576 distances
  for inspecting very tall maps.

## Quality assurance

* The automated suite grows to 586 tests, including UDMF extreme-height
  round-trips, per-format height-range checks, binary save-overflow warning
  coverage, grid theme contrast measurements, and measurement formatting.
